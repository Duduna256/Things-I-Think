Questions people ask

How does paying with MTN MoMo or Airtel Money work?

Pick MTN MoMo or Airtel Money when you book and you'll get a prompt on your phone. Enter your PIN and the money is held by reClain until your service is done. If your pro doesn't show, you get it all back.

Can my stylist come to my home?

Look for the “Home visits” tag. Choose “At my place” when you book, add your address and your pro brings the tools. A travel fee of UGX 10,000 is shown before you confirm.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel or move your booking for free up to 4 hours before. After that the venue may keep a small fee, because they turned someone else away for you.

Who checks the professionals?

The reClain team checks every business before it can take a single booking. Each business picks its own staff and is responsible for them. Security gets an extra rule: we only list security businesses that are registered companies.

How does laundry pickup work?

Book a pickup slot and a rider collects from your door. Wash & fold and wash & iron come back the next day. Express orders collected by 10:00 come back the same evening.
