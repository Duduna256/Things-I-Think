Frequently asked questions

Do stylists bring their own products?

Yes. At-home stylists bring tools and standard products. Braiding hair and extensions can be supplied by the stylist (added to your quote) or you can provide your own.

How do I pay?

Pay with MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, card, or cash after your service. You only pay once the job is done.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Free changes up to 4 hours before your booking. Late cancellations may carry a small fee that goes to the professional.

Is there an extra charge for home visits?

A small travel fee may apply, depending on distance. It is always shown before you confirm.

How are professionals vetted?

Every pro goes through an ID check, a skills assessment and reference checks before they can take bookings.
